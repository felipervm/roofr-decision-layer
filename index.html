04 / A LOW-COST TEST

How I would validate it with the team.

Start with the workflows Roofr already supports. Test whether surfacing exceptions improves the quality and speed of follow-through — without adding unnecessary work for contractors.

WEEK 01

Define “needs attention”

Align with product and support on what counts as inactivity, missing context and owner-assigned next steps. Review false positives.

WEEK 02

Measure baseline quality

Track missing next steps, unresolved exceptions and missing lost-job reasons. Segment by pipeline stage and lead source.

WEEK 03–04

Test a decision-ready review

Compare time-to-resolution and exception completion before and after a simple review workflow. Keep the contractor experience lightweight.

Success means fewer unresolved exceptions and faster follow-through, not just a prettier chart.
